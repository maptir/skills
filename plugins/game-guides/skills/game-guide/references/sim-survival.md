# Sim, survival, crafting, farming, exploration

## Where the irreversible decisions hide

The scarce resource here is usually **time**, not materials:

- **Calendar pressure.** Seasons, festivals, growth cycles, weather windows.
  Content that only exists during a window and returns a full cycle later — or
  never. A planting missed by one day costs a whole season.
- **Relationship and reputation gates.** Gifts, dialogue and events on a clock;
  some events only fire before a threshold is passed, so raising a stat too fast
  can skip content.
- **One-time resources on a permanent map.** Ore veins, ruins, rare spawns.
  Spending them on the wrong recipe is a real loss.
- **Tech and workshop ordering.** Unlocking in the wrong order means grinding
  for something the next tier would have trivialised.
- **Base placement**, in games where moving is expensive or impossible.

## Numbers to hunt

Length of a day/season/year and what resets on the boundary · growth times
versus season length (the deadline that catches everyone) · what each
relationship level unlocks and the rate you can raise it · recipe costs against
where the inputs come from · which resources are renewable versus finite ·
carry/storage capacity and when it stops being the bottleneck.

## Tabs that usually earn their place

The loop and the budget (the daily loop and what wastes time) · First-week and first-season
plan with dates · Calendar of windows and deadlines · Time-efficiency: what
compounds and what is a trap · Tech/tool priority · People — who to invest in
and what it unlocks · Finite resources and where they are · What to safely
ignore on a first run.

## Advice traps

- **Dates beat priorities.** "Plant this before day 24 or lose the season" is
  the shape that helps. A ranked list of crops is not.
- **Do not optimise the fun out.** These games are often played for texture, not
  throughput. Say which optimisations matter for keeping the run healthy and
  which are only for players chasing efficiency, and let them choose.
- **Guard against relationship spoilers.** Character events are story. Reference
  people by role and heart/level thresholds, keep names and event content behind
  the spoiler gate.
- **Name the early bottleneck explicitly.** In most of these games one specific
  constraint dominates the first several hours — inventory space, energy, a
  single tool tier. Saying which one, and what removes it, is the most useful
  sentence in the guide.
